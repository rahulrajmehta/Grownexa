How Agency Fees & Ad Budgets Work Together

To ensure complete trust, we always distinguish between two separate budget components:

1. Agency Management Fee

Paid directly to Imax Media for professional labor, strategy formulation, graphic creation, video editing, daily campaign monitoring, and reporting.

2. Advertising / Media Spend

Paid directly to advertising platforms (e.g. Meta Ads, Google Ads) through your own linked credit card or billing account. This money goes directly to auction views and clicks.

We do not take undisclosed markups on your media spend. You retain 100% visibility over your ad accounts and receipts issued directly by the ad platforms.
